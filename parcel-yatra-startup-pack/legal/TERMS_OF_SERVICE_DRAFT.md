## 2. What Parcel Yatra is — and is not

2.1 Parcel Yatra is a **technology platform** that helps a sender find a traveller who is already
travelling between two cities and who is willing to carry an eligible parcel.

2.2 **Parcel Yatra is not a courier, transporter, carrier or bailee.** We do not own or operate vehicles,
do not employ travellers, and do not take physical custody of any parcel.

2.3 The contract of carriage is between the **sender and the traveller**. Parcel Yatra's contract with
each of them is for platform services only (matching, verification, coordination, payment facilitation,
dispute handling).

2.4 Parcel Yatra does not guarantee that a match will be found, that a traveller will accept, or that a
parcel will be delivered by a particular time.

> **[COUNSEL: confirm 2.2–2.3 survive scrutiny given that we provide OTP custody evidence and adjudicate
> disputes. See LEGAL_REGULATORY_MATRIX B-1.]**

## 3. Eligibility

3.1 You must be at least **18 years** old and legally capable of contracting.

3.2 You must provide accurate information, including a mobile number you control.

3.3 Travellers must complete identity verification before accepting a parcel.

3.4 We may refuse, suspend or terminate access at our discretion for safety, legal or fraud reasons.

## 4. Sender obligations

4.1 You must accurately describe the contents, weight, dimensions and value of the parcel.

4.2 You must not send any item listed as **Prohibited** in our Prohibited & Restricted Goods Policy, and
must obtain approval for any **Restricted** item.

4.3 You must package the parcel securely and seal it in a tamper-evident manner.

4.4 You must be present (or arrange a named representative) at the agreed pickup point and time.

4.5 You must provide the pickup OTP only to the traveller, at handover, after inspecting the parcel.

4.6 You are responsible for the accuracy of the receiver's name and phone number, and you confirm you
have the receiver's consent to share those details for delivery purposes.
> **[COUNSEL: receiver consent basis — see PRIVACY_DATA_PROTECTION §12.5.]**

## 5. Traveller obligations

5.1 You must have a genuine, pre-planned journey matching the trip you publish.

5.2 You must not open, inspect beyond the sealed exterior, tamper with, or use the parcel.

5.3 You must carry the parcel with reasonable care and keep it in your custody from pickup to delivery.

5.4 You must not carry any parcel that appears to contain a prohibited item, and may refuse handover
without penalty.

5.5 You must complete delivery within the agreed window, or promptly notify the platform of any delay.

5.6 You are an **independent user of the platform**, not an employee, agent or worker of Parcel Yatra.
You control when and whether you travel and accept parcels.
> **[COUNSEL: worker-classification risk — see LEGAL_REGULATORY_MATRIX F-1.]**

5.7 You are responsible for complying with all applicable laws relating to your vehicle, licence,
insurance and the carriage of goods.
