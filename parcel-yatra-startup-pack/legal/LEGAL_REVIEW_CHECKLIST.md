# Legal Review Checklist

**Purpose:** a single, actionable brief for qualified Indian counsel. **Not legal advice.**
**Status:** Draft v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Estimated review scope:** 12–16 hours of counsel time.

---

## 0. What we are asking counsel to do

1. Tell us **whether the car-based peer carriage model is lawful** as designed.
2. Tell us **what we must not say or promise** in our terms, marketing and refund policy.
3. Tell us **which obligations attach now vs. on 13 May 2027** under the DPDP framework.
4. Identify anything in this pack that is **wrong, risky or missing**.

## 1. Must-answer questions (blocking launch)

| # | Question | Why blocking | Our provisional position |
|---|---|---|---|
| L-1 | Is one-off peer parcel carriage for reward on a private car trip lawful without a goods-carriage permit in UP/Delhi? | If no, the model cannot launch | Believed plausible; unconfirmed |
| L-2 | Does marketplace framing insulate us from carrier/bailee liability given OTP custody evidence and dispute adjudication? | Determines liability exposure and insurance | Believed partially; needs drafting |
| L-3 | Is the liability limitation in the ToS enforceable against consumers? | Without it, exposure is unbounded | Uncertain |
| L-4 | Can a licensed payment provider hold funds until delivery and pay a private traveller? | Without it, no money can move | Unconfirmed |
| L-5 | What is the GST/TDS position on platform fee and traveller payout? | Wrong treatment = penalties | Unresolved |
| L-6 | Is there any insurer that covers goods in peer custody? At what cost/exclusions? | Determines whether we can launch uninsured | Unknown |
| L-7 | What must the privacy notice contain, in which languages, and by when? | DPDP + consumer law | Drafted, unverified |
| L-8 | Do we need to appoint a DPO and/or publish a grievance officer? | Compliance + consumer rules | Grievance officer planned |

## 3. Facts counsel should verify independently (we could not)

- Current text of the **DPDP Act 2023** and **DPDP Rules 2025** (we relied on secondary summaries).
- The **commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E)** and the 13 May 2027 date.
- Current **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** obligations.
- Whether **IT Rules 2021** intermediary duties apply to us.
- **MoRTH/state** position on peer parcel carriage.
- **Indian Railways** parcel/luggage rules.
- **RBI** payment-aggregator directions regarding delayed settlement to individuals.
- Whether any **state platform-worker law** applies in UP/Delhi.

## 4. Risk register for counsel

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Our current mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model classified as unlicensed transport operation | Medium | Fatal | Narrow pilot, volume caps, counsel memo |
| Consumer liability claim exceeding cap | Medium | High | Explicit terms, dispute process, goodwill fund |
| Payment provider refuses onboarding | Medium | Fatal | Multiple provider conversations, sandbox-first |

| Data breach of ID documents | Low | Very High | Encryption, restricted access, retention limits |
| Tax assessment | Medium | Medium | Tax opinion before real money |
